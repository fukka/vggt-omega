# H6 novelty check — 2026-08-27 (abstracts only)

- **Foveation-Guided Dynamic Token Selection (FDT)**
  ([arXiv:2607.09480](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.09480v1)) — foveated token
  selection for ViT efficiency; concentrates compute at the FIXATION/center,
  mimicking human vision. Ours is the inversion: compute to the lens
  periphery, because the measured fisheye failure (fusion-starved rim,
  ticket 024B) is the opposite of the perceptual prior. Good contrast to cite.
- **StableDPT** ([arXiv:2601.02793](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.02793v1)) —
  temporal cross-attention into a DPT head for video depth; all-token,
  keyframe-global, no camera geometry. Closest video-depth mechanism; ours
  differs by lens-defined query restriction + measured justification.
- Factorized spatio-temporal attention (video transformers, generic) — the
  efficiency baseline family; none is camera/geometry-conditioned.

As far as these searches show, "temporal attention restricted to the
lens-defined periphery, justified by a measured give/receive asymmetry" is
unclaimed territory. TODO before submission: verify both papers to the
survey's standard.
