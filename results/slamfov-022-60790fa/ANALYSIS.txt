slamfov-022 -- ANALYSIS (radial half)
==============================================================================

THE QUESTION.  fovbench established on a synthetic twin that depth degrades
toward the rim of a wide lens.  This asks it of the real thing: does the same
degradation appear on real egocentric footage, scored against MPS SLAM points?

WHY IT NEEDS A CONTROL.  On this data distance and eccentricity move together
hard -- the rim of this lens looks at nearer surfaces, and every metric here is
relative.  So a bare error-against-eccentricity curve is partly a distance
curve.  The oracle arm answers per point from the ground truth with a fixed
0.15 m error -- the SAME error at every eccentricity and every distance, a model
with no field dependence whatever.  Whatever slope it shows is confound.

`spread` = largest live bin over smallest, on AbsRel.  `pooled` is every point
in the bin; `standardised` averages each bin over the distance strata all bins
share, so a bin cannot look good merely by being nearer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE NULL -- what a model with NO field dependence reads
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  oracle  aea       pooled 1.63x   standardised 1.10x   share 1.000
  oracle  nymeria   pooled 2.84x   standardised 1.26x   share 1.000

  A 1.63x (aea) / 2.84x (nymeria) "the rim is worse" effect, from a model that
  has no idea where the rim is.  Standardising removes most of it -- and the
  share row is 1.000 in every bin, so nothing was narrowed to achieve that.
  The 1.10-1.26x that survives is the coarseness of five strata, not a finding.

  So the NULL for the standardised column is ~1.10x on aea and ~1.26x on
  nymeria.  A real model must beat that to have said anything.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ANSWER -- the five models, standardised spread against that null
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  model        dataset     pooled  standard    null  over null
  vggt_omega   aea           2.13      1.83    1.10      1.67x
  vggt_1b      aea           2.09      1.80    1.10      1.64x
  da3_large    aea           1.85      1.59    1.10      1.45x
  da3_small    aea           1.92      1.56    1.10      1.42x
  dav2_large   aea           1.46      1.41    1.10      1.29x

  vggt_omega   nymeria       2.26      1.96    1.26      1.56x
  vggt_1b      nymeria       2.56      2.07    1.26      1.65x
  da3_large    nymeria       2.20      1.68    1.26      1.34x
  da3_small    nymeria       2.10      1.57    1.26      1.25x
  dav2_large   nymeria       1.35      1.57    1.26      1.25x

  READING.  Every model's standardised spread exceeds the oracle null, on both
  datasets.  The rim degradation on real egocentric footage is REAL: it survives
  holding GT distance fixed, at full share (1.000 in every bin -- no bin was
  narrowed to a slice).  fovbench's synthetic finding reproduces on real data
  with independent ground truth.

  But the pooled column is where the honesty is.  Read pooled, nymeria vggt_1b
  is a 2.56x field effect; standardised it is 2.07x against a 1.26x null.  The
  pooled row on this dataset is not a stronger version of the result, it is a
  different claim, and roughly half of what it reports is the subject changing
  rather than the model failing.

  AND THE CORRECTION DOES NOT ONLY EVER LOWER.  dav2_large on nymeria reads
  1.35x pooled and 1.57x standardised -- the confound was HIDING its field
  effect, not inventing one, because its errors happen to fall where the near
  surfaces are.  So "pooled overstates the rim penalty" is not a rule that can
  be applied as a discount; the confound has a sign per cell and only the
  two-way table recovers it.  This is the strongest argument in this artefact
  for not reading the pooled row alone.

  ORDERING.  dav2_large is the flattest field on both datasets (1.41x / 1.57x)
  while being far from the best in absolute terms.  vggt_omega has the best
  absolute AbsRel of the five on both datasets and one of the STEEPEST fields
  (1.83x / 1.96x).  Accuracy at the centre and evenness across the field are not
  the same axis, and a single pooled number hides which one a model bought.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Raw arm only.  --baselines raw deliberately: the two arms do not resample
    alike and the difference runs WITH eccentricity (raw decimates through
    INTER_AREA, which filters; rect resamples through INTER_LINEAR, which does
    not, by a factor running 2.13x on axis to 0.78x at 55 deg).  That asymmetry
    is shaped like the effect being measured, so mixing arms here would be
    unreadable.  Recorded, not fixed.
  * The window protocol -- where the camera was POINTED, as against where in
    the image -- is a separate arm and was still running when this was written.
  * Five distance strata.  The residual null (1.10x / 1.26x) is strata
    coarseness; --depth-strata 6+ would lower it and lower every "over null"
    figure's denominator.  The ordering is unaffected.
  * Nothing here is cross-box comparable to a lambda_63 number at better than
    ~1.3% AbsRel; see results/slambench-022sc-b961f98 for that control.
