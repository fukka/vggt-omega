slambench-022sc-b961f98 -- ANALYSIS
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS IS.  Six slambench.run shards produced on the space-container A100 pod
on 2026-08-20, against ego-synth 5B (aea + nymeria), split digest 61195914f090 --
the same split #020 published.  They sat in the pod's eval_out unpublished until
2026-08-22; findings.md already cites their stride-10 result, so the numbers were
read before the artefacts were on a branch.  This directory closes that gap.

It carries two things #020 does not:

  1. a CROSS-BOX CONTROL.  84 of its cells are #020's cells, recomputed on a
     different GPU architecture (A100-80GB vs lambda_63's RTX 6000 Ada).
  2. 48 NEW cells -- the multi-frame context arms at STRIDE 1, which #020 never
     ran.  #020 has N=1 at stride 1 and N=3/5/10 at stride 10 only.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CROSS-BOX CONTROL -- the 84 cells that are also #020's
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  AbsRel  n=84  mean|rel| 0.1506%   max|rel| 1.2788%   at ('da3_small', 'nymeria', 'rect_derect', 5, 10)
  RMSE    n=84  mean|rel| 0.0376%   max|rel| 0.2927%   at ('da3_small', 'nymeria', 'rect_derect', 5, 10)
  SqRel   n=84  mean|rel| 0.1914%   max|rel| 1.1017%   at ('da3_small', 'nymeria', 'rect_derect', 10, 10)

  Not bit-identical, and it was never going to be: a different GPU architecture
  reorders floating-point reductions.  The size is what matters.  Mean 0.15% and
  worst 1.28% on AbsRel is an order below the 3-40% context effects this harness
  is used to measure, so a pod number may be tabulated beside a lambda one --
  but it is NOT zero, so the two boxes must not be mixed inside a single curve.
  Why da3_small nymeria rect_derect is the worst cell is not established here.

  stride 1  : 48 arms, ratio 0.957-1.157, mean 1.022
  stride 10 : 48 arms, ratio 0.867-1.086, mean 0.977

  READING.  At stride 1 the mean arm is slightly WORSE than its own single frame
  and 10 frames is usually the worst point of the three -- adding adjacent frames
  costs rather than pays.  At stride 10 the mean arm improves and the best cells
  reach -13% (da3_large nymeria rect_derect at 10 frames, 0.867).

  The mechanism this is consistent with is baseline, not frame count: consecutive
  frames of a head-mounted camera are separated by ~1 cm, which is no parallax
  against several metres of scene, so the extra views add compute and no geometry.
  Stride 10 buys ~9 cm and the same frame count then pays.

  This is the same conclusion #001 recorded for ScanNet++ stride, reached
  independently on real egocentric footage with MPS SLAM points as ground truth,
  and it is what autoresearch/findings.md cites under H11's temporal re-scope.
