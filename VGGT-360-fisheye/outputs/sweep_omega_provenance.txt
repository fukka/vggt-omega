*** These numbers use a superseded cone mask. ***

They predate cc6d1a13 ("fisheye: mask to the lens' usable FOV, not the KB4
turnover", 2026-08-05) by six days, so theta_max came from the KB4 fold-back
turnover (62.33 deg) rather than the lens' actual imaged cone (54.83 deg). By
that commit's own measurements the old cutoff meant ~5-12% of every ring view's
"valid" pixels were dead vignette handed to VGGT flagged as valid, and 10.9% of
the eval mask was scored against GT with a black input.

Issue #7 records three conclusions resting on this file: the FoV peak at
100 deg, raw_roi being worse everywhere, and the FoV-error/edge-alignment
correlation (r = -0.759, n=1). All three were computed on the old mask.

Block E (sweep_f2/f20/f60/f120/f200) re-runs this sweep on five frames with the
corrected mask. It therefore supersedes this file on BOTH counts -- sample size
and mask -- and align% here is not directly comparable to align% there.
